TCSV, highlighted

Each example in tree-sitter/examples, through the TextMate grammar (syntaxes/tcsv.tmLanguage.json) and Shiki, the engine GitHub and VS Code share. The themes know nothing of TCSV: every colour comes from a scope they already paint.

A column's name and its text cells share a colour, six in turn; numbers, dates, booleans, links and quoted text keep their own. In the header, the type, each mark, constraint and choice wear their own scope; the prelude is its own region. use and references like accounts.account are provisional, from SPEC's Open section.
