@README.md

**"Programmable data." is the line, the hero's and the product's.** Never reword it, shorten it or put another line in its place: every place that says what Cells is leads with those two words (the site's hero and title, the README, the link card, a description), read from `web/lib/product.ts`'s `tagline`. The landing's hero is not to be changed.

**Cells is not meant to be compatible with CSV.** Adopting it is renaming a `.csv` to `.cells`, and that is the only bridge. Never weigh a design by how CSV tools (pandas, spreadsheets, DuckDB) would read a Cells file, never offer it as a cost or a benefit, and never bend a rule to keep them reading it: that road has no bottom.

This repository is public: every example is invented, and nothing about a person's life, accounts or private repositories goes in it. Every Markdown file is indexed in README.md's "The documents"; a new one is added there in the same commit.
